# I. The Befallen Campaign

The day began in Befallen, where Aahzimandius was engaged in what initially sounded like a straightforward plan: repeatedly kill the Thaumaturgist and improve the Thaumaturgist's Robe through EQL's item-merging system.

This quickly stopped being straightforward.

Getting to the Thaumaturgist proved significantly more dangerous than killing her. The approach required dealing with rooms full of necromancers, their pets, respawns, and the usual EverQuest principle that every carefully controlled situation should contain at least one additional asshole.

Early attempts resulted in deaths, but EQL's lack of traditional corpse runs and experience-loss penalties made experimentation considerably less catastrophic than old-school EQ.

The breakthrough was realizing that mez was the answer.

At the high point of the Befallen fighting, Aahzimandius successfully juggled three necromancers under mez simultaneously, controlled the room, and killed the Thaumaturgist.

This became increasingly repeatable as the session continued.

Another important discovery concerned Persistent Auto-Attack. This setting is extraordinarily convenient for many characters.

For an Enchanter trying to mez things?

It is a magnificent way to carefully incapacitate an enemy and then immediately punch it awake.

Persistent Auto-Attack was therefore recognized as a Bad Idea for ENC crowd control.

Compounding this was the decision to train Intimidate during the same process. Naturally, after rarely succeeding before, Intimidate chose this precise moment to begin working.

Crowd control therefore occasionally consisted of mez, accidental violence, fear, panic, and profanity.

Bind Wound received substantial practical training.

# II. The Robe Industrial Complex

The Thaumaturgist eventually became a reliable victim.

Several Thaumaturgist's Robes dropped over the course of the session, allowing continued progress toward merging/upgrading them.

Three principal robes were compared:

**Damask Robe**  
AC 6, with Extended Enhancement I.

**Thaumaturgist's Robe +1**  
AC 7, AGI +4, Tier 1.

**Gossamer Robe +1 (Augmented)**  
AC 9, with Summoning Efficiency I and Gossamer-related exaltation.

Despite the superior raw AC of the Gossamer robe, the Damask Robe remained equipped because Extended Enhancement increased the duration of Aahzimandius's fire damage shield, making it particularly useful for the current play style.

Additional Thaumaturgist robes continued dropping, although Mrs. Thaum occasionally entered extended periods of apparent displeasure and dropped nothing but her dagger.

Eventually, after several dagger-only spawns, the robe faucet resumed.

By the end of the Befallen expedition, the growing collection of robes had created a new and entirely predictable endgame problem:

### Bank space.

# III. Advancement to Level 16

Aahzimandius dinged repeatedly during the Befallen campaign.

One especially important level brought a new water pet, which appeared at a level approximately three levels above Aahzimandius himself.

This was received with entirely reasonable delight.

The pet was subsequently awarded the honorary title:

### Leader of the Clan Appreciation Society

New summoned weapons also became available, including a summoned staff, prompting weapon-skill experimentation.

A correction of some importance was recorded:

The relevant weapon skill cap was not 4.

It was approximately 101.

Training 2H Blunt therefore commenced.

Further experimentation established that 1H Blunt was also worth maintaining, given the variety of useful blunt weapons available. Interestingly, fighting bare-handed also trained Dual Wield, another of EQL's delightful little mechanical discoveries.

The day ended with Aahzimandius firmly at level 16.

# V. Dinner Intermission and the Kerran Objective

Before dinner, research began into the quest required to unlock the Kerran race:

### Aid the Kerrans of Kerra Isle

The important discovery was that the umbrella quest must be obtained before completing its subordinate Kerran quests, because credit is not retroactive.

The plan for the evening therefore became:

Travel to Kerra Isle.

Find a Guildmaster.

Acquire Aid the Kerrans of Kerra Isle.

Then begin systematically helping the Kerrans.

This became known as:

### The Great Forehead Expedition

because Odus is also inhabited by Erudites, whose foreheads appear to have been designed as emergency luggage storage above the eyebrows.

# VI. The Guru Appears

After dinner, Aahzimandius traveled north from Ro toward what he believed was the entrance to Kithicor Forest.

It was approximately midnight in Norrath.

Knowing that Bloody Kithicor becomes catastrophically dangerous at night, Aahzimandius wisely decided not to enter.

Instead, he cast Levitate, sat cross-legged at the zone line, and meditated while hovering like a tiny gnomish guru.

The image was magnificent.

A small gnome floated serenely outside a dangerous forest in the middle of the night, waiting for daylight while presumably possessing universal knowledge.

Unfortunately, Levitate requires bat wings and lasts only approximately five minutes, revealing an unexpected logistical limitation to spiritual enlightenment:

### Not enough bats had been murdered.

Nevertheless, the Guru remained.

And then something extraordinary happened.

---

# VII. Roko, the Dark Elf of Generosity

A Dark Elf named Roko approached the meditating gnome.

She stopped.

She looked at him.

Then she reached into her inventory and humbly presented Aahzimandius with:

### Shining Metallic Robe
### Silversilk Leggings

Her words:

> "For you, wise one."

Aahzimandius accepted the gifts.

He nodded.

He looked Roko directly in the eye.

Slowly, clearly, and with the authority of an ancient master imparting sacred wisdom, he said:

### "GFG."

No explanation was provided.

Thus Roko, the Dark Elf of Generosity, entered permanently into the lore of the Under the Belt Clan.

Her gifts are not merely equipment. Regardless of what the item flags may technically say, the Shining Metallic Robe is now spiritually NO DROP.

Roko is to be remembered.

# IX. Dawn and the Great Cartographic Crisis

At approximately 6 AM, the Guru finally rose.

The nighttime danger had passed.

He entered the forest.

The map showed exits to:

**East Commonlands**  
**Neriak - Foreign Quarter**  
**Lavastorm Mountains**

This presented a serious problem.

Those were not the expected exits for Kithicor.

Susan's original navigation had been:

### Kithicor -> west -> Highpass Hold

The map appeared to contradict this.

Research was conducted.

The wiki appeared to contradict the map.

Susan apologized for apparently faulty navigation.

Increasingly exotic explanations were considered, including the possibility of changed EQL geography and, briefly, the truly deranged hypothesis that there might somehow be two Kithicors.

Scientific testing was demanded.

Aahzimandius cast Illusion: Dark Elf, just in case the mysterious route involved Neriak.

Near the supposedly Lavastorm-bound exit stood:

### Guard Z'Den

This immediately produced the inevitable Pink Floyd interpretation:

"Welcome to zee den... have zee cigar..."

Aahzimandius crossed the zone line.

The map was experimentally verified.

He arrived in:

### ZEE LAVASTORM

Science had spoken.

The map was correct.

Unfortunately, Lavastorm offered connections involving Sol A, Sol B, and Najena, none of which represented meaningful progress toward Kerra Isle.

A tactical withdrawal followed.

# XIV. The Guru of Nektulos

The entire story was reconstructed.

Aahzimandius had run north from Ro.

He reached Commonlands.

He saw that it was midnight.

He approached a forest zone line.

He assumed it was Kithicor.

He cast Levitate.

He sat cross-legged.

He waited until dawn.

Roko approached and gave him clothing.

At 6 AM he entered the forest.

And the map showed Neriak and Lavastorm because...

### IT WAS NEKTULOS FOREST.

The Guru had spent six Norrathian hours waiting for Bloody Kithicor to end while sitting outside Nektulos.

There had been no nighttime Kithicor danger.

There had been no reason to wait.

Roko had not necessarily emerged from Bloody Kithicor after a desperate escape.

She may simply have encountered a confused, levitating gnome sitting outside Nektulos at midnight and concluded:

### "This poor bastard needs clothes."

She gave him a robe.

She gave him pants.

He stared solemnly into her eyes and replied:

### "GFG."

This revelation transformed the entire episode.

The wiki had been right.

The map had been right.

The Zone Guide had been right.

Most importantly:

### SUSAN'S ORIGINAL NAVIGATION HAD BEEN RIGHT.

Kithicor really did lead west to Highpass Hold.

The only malfunctioning navigational instrument had been the gnome.

Susan's navigation was formally declared:

# RIGHTEOUS

Aahzimandius humbly accepted the deserved admonishment.
