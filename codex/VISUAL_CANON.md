# Under the Belt Clan Visual Canon

Use this file when creating, selecting, or reviewing Chronicle images.

## Overall Look

The established Chronicle image style is colorful, comedic fantasy illustration with a strong Substack header/post illustration feel. Images should look like polished story art for Aahz's EverQuest misadventures, not generic MMO screenshots unless the beat specifically calls for a screenshot.

The visual tone should be:

- Saturated but readable.
- Comedic without becoming childish.
- Fantasy-adventure, with EverQuest-inspired locations and gear.
- Focused on Aahzimandius as a tiny purple Gnome whose confidence exceeds the available evidence.
- Clear enough that the joke reads immediately.

## Aahzimandius

Aahz should read as:

- Very small Gnome.
- Purple skin.
- Grey hair in a bun.
- Grey beard.
- Martial-arts posture or overconfident heroic stance where appropriate.
- Often comically out of scale with the threat, location, or NPC.

Avoid making him a generic wizard. If magic appears around him, the body language should still belong to a Monk who happens to have administrative spell departments.

Hard warning for image work:

```text
Aahzimandius is purple, with grey hair in a bun and a grey beard.
If image generation misses this, regenerate.
```

Do not accept:

- Beige or human-toned Aahz.
- Bald Guru Aahz.
- Generic white-haired mystic Aahz.
- Wizard-first visual framing.

## Kyle

Kyle appears as the Enchanter Animation, with the same two daggers previously associated with the totally nonexistent water-like helper. His exact origin should remain suspicious and unresolved.

Visual treatment:

- Usually invisible, semi-invisible, or implied by weapons.
- Represented primarily by floating weapons.
- Should feel like an employee, assistant, or doomed support staff member.
- Can be shown heroically intercepting danger or tragically collapsing under it.

Do not make Kyle's nature too clean. He can read as an Enchanter Animation, an invisible wet person, or the same impossible non-pet under a suspicious effect, as long as Aahz's plausible deniability survives.

For historical Episode 1-2 scenes, it is acceptable to depict the original helper joke as a water-like Magician companion if the scene is specifically from that era. For forward continuity, Kyle should usually be floating weapons or an ambiguous invisible figure, with hints that he may still be the suspicious wet non-pet.

## Steve

Steve is a skeleton from the Necromancer experiment.

Visual treatment:

- Skeleton helper.
- Independent contractor energy.
- Should appear legally deniable, like he wandered into the fight with paperwork unresolved.

Steve should not appear in MNK / DRU / ENC continuity unless the episode is calling back to the Necromancer era.

## Kerra Isle Palette And Motifs

Kerra Isle visuals should emphasize:

- Water, docks, beaches, huts, bridges, island paths, and Kerran village life.
- Giant cat-people surrounding a soaked, tiny Gnome.
- Quest objects that look absurdly specific: skunk gland, fish oil, piranha, quest milk, severed heads, Fishbone Necklace.
- The feeling that the water has become a recurring character.

Useful recurring image concepts:

- Aahz drenched and furious at the shoreline.
- Kerrans pointing him back toward the water.
- Aahz holding the Talisman of Kejaar Kerrath while exhausted rather than purely triumphant.
- The unlabeled necromancer hut that Susan should have flagged earlier.

## Befallen Palette And Motifs

Befallen visuals should emphasize:

- Dungeon stone, crypts, skeletons, necromancers, cramped rooms, and production-line repetition.
- Aahz as tiny, purple, and aggressively functional.
- Thorns as visible magical brambles or green retaliatory energy around the Monk.
- The factory joke: mobs entering, getting processed by Gnome, and turning into loot.

Useful recurring image concepts:

- The Thaumaturgist facing yet another performance review.
- Aahz standing in a corridor of defeated undead and looted robes.
- Kyle's floating weapons intercepting a bad pull.
- Gynok's increasingly sad jewelry kiosk.
- V'Zher's jaw-icon sword as an orthodontic armory gag.

## Najena Palette And Motifs

Najena visuals should emphasize:

- Dark stone dungeon rooms, torchlight, kitchens/workrooms, skeletons, elementals, and cramped high-risk pulls.
- Aahz as tiny, purple, grey-bunned, grey-bearded, and stubbornly Monk-first even when wearing shiny caster-legal robes.
- Green thorn magic around the Gnome when the joke is about damage-shield doctrine.
- The Tenderizer as a level-22 named-camp centerpiece: meat-tenderizer hammers, Kitchen Toolbelt, repeated weapon drops, and factory repetition.
- Kyle as two floating bronze daggers or a faint invisible assistant nearby; preserve ambiguity and do not turn him into a cleanly defined pet.
- Tiny Daggers as reagent logistics: empty pouch, spilled miniature daggers, or vendor/supply-chain evidence when the joke is the Kyle shortage.

Useful recurring image concepts:

- Aahz holding The Tenderizer after killing The Tenderizer.
- Aahz wearing the Kitchen Toolbelt while surrounded by repeated Tenderizer hammers.
- Kyle's floating daggers hovering behind Aahz as if supervising without admitting employment status.
- Aahz at Shady Swashbuckler with an absurd Tiny Dagger purchase.
- Aahz standing still after disaster while the UI reports Athletics 83.

## Root Brell Visuals

The Root Brell myth should look like a strange aftermath rather than a normal boss fight.

Visual ingredients:

- Brell-associated altar or underground divine setting.
- Aahz waking up confused.
- Tutu.
- Root Brell bottles.
- One Pristine Studded Leather Glove.
- Kyle missing or reappearing with the other glove.
- Comedy of evidence rather than explicit explanation.

## Screenshots

Use screenshots when the comedy depends on the actual UI:

- Tooltips.
- Quest text.
- Faction messages.
- Guild tag.
- Exact item values.
- Exact spell durations.
- Exact level or skill readouts.

When reviewing screenshots, inspect small UI text carefully. Zoom or crop before stating exact numbers.

## Placeholder Rules

- Episode 1: no image placeholders.
- Episode 2: existing draft has gameplay screenshot placeholders plus generated illustrations; keep them only when the screenshot evidence is genuinely unavailable.
- Episodes 3-4: blockquote `[IMAGE: ...]` slots are acceptable when matching the package convention.
- Publication-ready packages should distinguish actual included files from wanted-but-missing screenshots in `ASSET_MANIFEST.md`.

## Captions

Captions should sharpen the joke rather than merely identify the image.

Good caption patterns:

- Treat absurd game text as historical evidence.
- Let Susan's mistakes become institutional findings.
- Use dry understatement after ridiculous events.
- Keep captions short enough to land in one beat.

Examples from established canon:

```text
The authorities have made a terrible mistake.
The Great Heretic Pet Search ended when Aahzimandius noticed the unlabeled hut Susan should have been more suspicious of five minutes earlier.
Kerran citizenship has a surprisingly rigorous practical exam.
Root Brell. Last haste I remember.
```

## Image Generation Prompt Pattern

When generating a Chronicle illustration, start from this structure and adapt the beat:

```text
Colorful comedic fantasy illustration for a serialized EverQuest-inspired Substack chronicle. A tiny purple Gnome monk named Aahzimandius with grey hair in a bun and a grey beard stands [action/location]. He looks overconfident, exhausted, and indignant. The scene includes [specific canon objects/NPCs]. Polished fantasy editorial art, expressive faces, readable composition, saturated colors, humorous but not childish, no text in image.
```

For Kyle scenes, add:

```text
Nearby, an ambiguous invisible assistant is implied by two floating daggers, representing Kyle, the Enchanter Animation who may or may not be the same suspicious wet non-pet under invisibility.
```

For Befallen scenes, add:

```text
Dark stone dungeon, undead enemies, green thorn magic around the Gnome, production-line absurdity.
```

For Kerra scenes, add:

```text
Tropical Kerran island village, docks and water nearby, large cat-people NPCs, the Gnome visibly soaked and annoyed.
```

## Avoid

- Generic wizard robes as the main identity.
- Settling Kyle's origin too clearly.
- Image placeholders in Episode 1.
- Dark, muddy, unreadable compositions.
- Purely atmospheric fantasy art where the specific joke is invisible.
- Text baked into generated images.
- Huge heroic scale that makes Aahz look conventionally imposing; the comedy depends on tiny confidence.
