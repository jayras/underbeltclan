# Under the Belt Clan Chronicle Style Guide

Use this guide when drafting or editing Chronicle prose.

## Voice

The voice is irreverent, specific, and mock-epic. It treats tiny game events as sacred historical disasters and treats absurd UI details as documentary evidence.

Keep:

- Aahz's profanity.
- Susan/Aahz banter.
- Exact chronology from the play session.
- Specific item names, NPC names, spell names, levels, skill values, and tooltip absurdities.
- Short punchline paragraphs after long setups.
- Over-serious institutional language applied to stupid events.

Avoid:

- Sanitizing the language.
- Smoothing the story into generic fantasy recap.
- Replacing specific beats with vague summaries.
- Making Aahz sound like a conventional hero.
- Making Susan purely incompetent; she is useful enough that the failures matter.

## Narrative Shape

Chronicles work best as a sequence of escalating discoveries.

Common structure:

1. Begin with a mock-heroic thesis for the session.
2. Re-establish current build, level, goal, and any embarrassing unresolved context.
3. Follow the actual play chronology.
4. Let mechanical discoveries become doctrine.
5. Let mistakes become named institutional failures.
6. End with a final accounting that lists what changed and what got worse in the funniest possible way.

The story should feel like the session happened, not like a summary of intended content.

## Canon Drift

The Chronicles are allowed to contain earlier misunderstandings. Do not sand them away.

When an older episode conflicts with newer canon:

- Preserve the older episode's wording if archiving or quoting it.
- Explain the contradiction as chronology when writing new material.
- Use the newest confirmed episode or current user correction for forward-facing canon.
- Add a guide note if the discrepancy will matter again.

Examples:

- Episode 1-2 use the early water-pet / Leader of the Clan Appreciation Society joke.
- Episode 4 complicates Kyle canon: Kyle is apparently the Enchanter Animation, but the same two daggers make him suspiciously like the earlier wet non-pet under invisibility.
- Episode 1 lists `the Kerran tser`; Episode 2 uses `tseq`. Use `tseq` for Episode 2 unless new screenshot evidence says otherwise.

## Paragraph Rhythm

Use a mix of:

- Normal narrative paragraphs.
- One-line emphasis.
- Repeated sentence fragments.
- Big standalone slogans.
- Short dialogue exchanges.
- Final-accounting lists.

The style often lands jokes by isolating the last turn:

```text
The reward was a Fishbone Necklace.

After all that.

Fish.
```

## Profanity

Profanity is part of Aahz's voice. Keep it when it is doing work.

Established forms include:

```text
Fuck Roary.
GET FUCKED, GNOME!
GFG: Get Fucked, Gnome.
Because fucking level 10.
Susan: wrong as fuck.
Not another fucking jaw.
```

Do not sprinkle profanity randomly. Use it as punctuation for disbelief, outrage, and EverQuest's ability to turn small errands into full spiritual collapses.

## Aahz And Susan

Aahz is the player-character voice: indignant, funny, grandiose, and constantly betrayed by logistics.

Susan is the AI assistant character: planning partner, researcher, navigator, accountability ledger, and occasional source of spectacularly wrong advice.

Their banter should preserve affection through exasperation. The joke is collaborative suffering, not cruelty.

Good Susan joke pattern:

- Susan gives advice.
- The game proves the advice wrong.
- The record updates with legalistic precision.
- Aahz survives and becomes more sarcastic.

## Episode 1 Handling

Episode 1 is the published/manual-origin foundation episode. Do not rewrite it from vibes.

When backfilling or referencing Episode 1:

- Treat the published Substack post as canonical source material.
- Preserve the original ENC / MNK / MAG era rather than forcing later MNK / DRU / ENC continuity backward.
- Preserve the original Leader of the Clan Appreciation Society joke while noting that Episode 4 makes Kyle's origin more ambiguous, not cleanly resolved.
- Keep "GFG: Get Fucked, Gnome," "In EQ, snakes kick," and "Sarcasm is SOP" available as foundation slogans.
- Use Episode 1 to explain why Kerra Isle begins as the next quest destination.
- Do not add image placeholders to Episode 1; it was text-only.
- Preserve its title style as singular `Under the Belt Clan Chronicle` when archiving the published article, even though later episodes use `Chronicles`.

## Mechanics As Comedy

Mechanics are funniest when precise.

Preserve exact observations:

- Episode 1 start/end: approximately level 12-13 to level 16 as ENC / MNK / MAG.
- Swimming increases from 6 to about 16 on the first Kerra crossing.
- Swimming 58.
- Level: fucking 10.
- Persistent Auto-Attack is bad for mez because it wakes targets.
- Weapon skill cap correction: not 4, approximately 101.
- Gynok worth about 5% XP.
- Mummies worth about 4% XP.
- Shield of Thistles duration examples: 4:36 base / 5:12 extended at level 12, 4:54 base / 5:36 extended at level 13.
- Quickness duration around 4:00, 4:36 with Extended Enhancement.
- High Guard of Erudin -300 and Kerra Isle positive faction shifts.
- Fishbone Necklace "Value: absolutely nothing."

If a fact comes from memory, a wiki, or inference rather than current screenshots/tooltips, label it as uncertain before using it as advice.

## Build Language

Always frame the build as Monk-first, but do not imply Aahz is unaware he is a caster. He knows he is using magic and enjoys using it to improve his Kung-Fu. The line is: magic augments the Monk, it does not replace the Monk.

Good:

```text
Aahzimandius does not stop being a Monk in order to become a Druid.
He becomes a Monk with automated departments.
```

Good:

```text
If magic fails, Monk remains the base operating system.
```

Avoid:

```text
Aahz uses Monk as a backup while casting.
```

The support magic exists so the punching can continue.

Pet-denial rule:

- Aahz never summons pets.
- Aahz has no pets.
- Pet mechanics may still happen nearby for reasons no one can prove.
- Aahz may accidentally mention summoning pets or putting a pet summon on the cast bar.
- Susan knows the truth and should respond with sarcastic "surprise."
- Aahz corrects her that no pet was summoned.
- Susan then theatrically "forgets" the evidence, because appeasing the Guru's denial is part of the bit.

## Headings

Headings should sound like episode beats, jokes, or doctrines rather than generic section labels.

Good heading examples:

```text
Because Fucking Level 10
Susan Attempts Geography
This Time, the Guru Does the Punching
The Thorny Gnome Doctrine
Kyle Goes to the Gym
The Ice Bone Skeleton School of Repetitive Cranial Trauma
The Weird Shit Spawn Point
The Brell Problem
```

Avoid bland headings like:

```text
Quest Progress
Combat Strategy
Loot Summary
Travel
```

## Final Accounting

Episodes should usually end with a final accounting. It should recap concrete progress and convert the session's absurdity into canon.

Include:

- Starting and ending level.
- Class changes.
- Major unlocks.
- Important items.
- Named enemies or NPCs.
- The main doctrine or joke established.
- Any permanent damage to Susan's reputation.

Final accounting should be funny, but it must also be useful continuity.

## Captions And Image Notes

Captions should have a joke or interpretive angle.

Image placeholders in drafts should be explicit when a screenshot is still needed:

```text
> [IMAGE: Screenshot of Aahzimandius with the new `<Under the Belt>` guild tag]
>
> Caption: The authorities have made a terrible mistake.
```

Generated-image placeholders should reference the actual image path once created.

## Editorial Rules

- Preserve chronology even when a cleaner order is tempting.
- Keep exact recurring beats intact.
- If a live page cannot be inspected, do not claim it was reviewed.
- For publication review, inspect exported HTML/PDF when direct Substack access fails.
- Check for unresolved placeholders such as `[Insert screenshot]`, `[Generate illustration]`, or broken relative image paths.
- When preparing a publishable draft, proactively generate matching visuals if the established visual style exists and the user has asked for a complete Chronicle.
