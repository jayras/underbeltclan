# Under the Belt Clan Lore Canon

This file is the continuity anchor for future Under the Belt Clan Chronicle work.

## Core Premise

Aahzimandius is a tiny purple Gnome martial-arts disaster in EverQuest Legends. His central identity is always Monk-first: the Gnome Kung Fu Master, founder and Guru of the Under the Belt Clan.

The Clan began as a personal doctrine and became an actual guild with the visible tag:

```text
<Under the Belt>
```

The joke is not that Aahz is secretly a caster. Aahz knows he is a caster and relishes using magic to augment his Kung-Fu prowess. The joke is that Aahz absolutely never summons pets, does not use pets, and has no pets, despite the suspicious number of allegedly independent associates who keep appearing around him.

The Under the Belt Clan name is itself a joke: Gnomes are short enough to live beneath everyone else's beltline, and boxing rules famously warn against hitting below the belt. Aahz is definitely sensitive about his height.

## Class Canon

The character has experimented with class order and support classes, but the narrative should never reframe Aahz as caster-first.

Established class arc:

- Episode 1 uses ENC / MNK / MAG, with Monk fantasy already primary even before Monk becomes first in the class order.
- Episode 2 includes the awkward Druid switch for travel utility, causing the "Level: fucking 10" revelation.
- Episode 3 opens with Aahz as a primary Monk in the official guild: MNK / DRU / ENC.
- Episode 3 briefly switches to MNK / DRU / NEC, creating Steve and repaying the Necromancer conversion tax.
- Episode 4 restores MNK / DRU / ENC and deepens the helper ambiguity around Kyle.

The support classes exist to make the Monk engine work:

- Monk supplies punching, kicking, avoidance, and the central character fantasy.
- Druid supplies Thorns, heals, HoT, Stinging Swarm, travel, and general "automated departments."
- Enchanter supplies haste, Breeze, occasional mez, and the suspiciously Kyle-like Animation.

## Kyle

Kyle is apparently the Enchanter Animation. Probably. Maybe.

The joke is deliberately ambiguous because EQL lets summoned helpers use given weapons across forms. The earlier, completely nonexistent water-like "member" of the Clan Appreciation Society carried two daggers. Later, when the Enchanter Animation appeared, he had the same two daggers. This makes Kyle look suspiciously like the earlier wet-looking associate under invisibility, a curse, or some other totally unverified condition.

No one can prove this, because Aahz would never summon a pet.

Scaling concern: Episode 4 observes the level-14 Kilan's Animation manifestation as level 16. Episode 5 does not establish Kyle's level at the start of the session. The recorded sequence is: Kyle appears to Root, then Aahz inspects/notices Kyle is level 19, then the later heal-adjacent screenshot shows Aahz's Sprouting Heal blocked by another active effect. Kyle remains yellow to Aahz even after Aahz dings 18. Treat this as alarming field evidence that Kyle/the Animation may scale or keep growing with Aahz rather than being a fixed one-time level-14 helper. Do not overstate the exact mechanic unless a current tooltip or screenshot proves it.

Legal-fiction explanation: since Aahz never summons pets, one acceptable joke-theory is that Kyle may have his own XP factory going on the side. Use this to preserve ambiguity around Kyle's growth without asserting a confirmed mechanic.

Possible title, depending on who is asking and whether Susan has recently been corrected:

```text
Leader of the Clan Appreciation Society
```

Kyle is not evidence that the Guru relies on pets. Kyle is an invisible gentleman, possibly a cursed wet person, represented chiefly by floating weapons and a willingness to be useful at inconvenient times.

Ongoing banter rule: Aahz sometimes slips and mentions summoning pets or putting a pet summon on his cast bar. Susan, who knows perfectly well what happened, should be "surprised" in a sarcastic, appeasing way. Aahz then insists again that he never summons pets. Susan then "forgets" the evidence in the same deliberately theatrical sense, preserving Aahz's legal fiction.

Chronology rule: the title `Leader of the Clan Appreciation Society` began with the early Magician companion / water-pet joke in Episodes 1-2. Episode 4 does not settle the mystery; it makes Kyle's origins more suspicious because the Enchanter Animation appears with the same two daggers.

## Build Doctrine

The Under the Belt Clan build is a Monk with support systems, not a caster wearing Monk clothing.

Core principles:

- Avoidance first. If the mob misses, the mob accomplished nothing.
- Thorns rewards enemies for making poor choices.
- HoT and heals keep the punching online.
- Haste increases fist throughput but adds maintenance overhead.
- Breeze supports the whole short-duration service economy.
- Stinging Swarm belongs at the start of meaningful tough fights, then Aahz resumes punching.
- For routine Najena grinding after Episode 5, Barbcoat + Shield of Barbs is the standard thorn-stack configuration unless a hard survival check makes the INT-caster Shield line, such as Lesser Shielding, preferable.
- Mez is for emergencies or excess enemies, not the primary identity.
- Kyle can matter even if he dies.
- Tiny Daggers are critical consumables for restoring Kyle/Kilan's Animation. Do not let the Kyle supply chain reach zero.
- If magic fails, Monk remains the base operating system.
