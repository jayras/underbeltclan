## Prose-Only Evidence

These beats are recorded in chat text but may not have a matching screenshot:

- Episode 8 begins hours before Susan receives the logs, creating the need for a pre-parsed Episode Telemetry Feed.
- The parser's first iteration buried major events, and corrected uploads became authoritative.
- The parser improvements include stable encounter IDs, explicit stack semantics, raw and normalized names, separated effects, and cross-file continuation.
- `Xarer = Kyle` is supported by the log's normalized/raw caster handling and the Courage cast.
- The user reports Regurgitated Thaumaturgist uses the ghoul model.
- The user believes about four Flaming Fists dropped by this point; corrected logs identify at least two additional Flaming Fist loot events after Episode 7.
- Episode 7's ding-kill Flaming Fist theorem is contradicted by Episode 8, but remains canonically "true" because Gnome Science likes it better.
- Tepid Deeds creates the Factory Mode versus OSHA Mode doctrine.
- Susan's Rathyl advice was strategically good but navigationally incomplete because it answered the coordinate question before the route-access question.
- The locked-door/key-progression chain is discovered but not completed in Episode 8.
